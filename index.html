This Privacy Policy details how our mobile application collects, uses, protects, and discloses information when you use our app.

By downloading, accessing, or using Aura Tarot, you agree to the terms outlined in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

A. Information You Provide Directly

  • Astrological Profile Data (Optional): You may optionally input your date of birth, time of birth, and place of birth. This data is strictly used on your device to calculate your astrological natal chart (Sun, Moon, Ascendant) and current planetary transits for personalized tarot readings.
  • Questions & Tarot Notes: Questions submitted to the oracle and any private interpretation notes you choose to save.
  • Photos & Camera Data: If you use the Photo Spread (Анализ по фото) feature, you may grant camera or photo library access to capture images of physical tarot cards on your table. These photos are processed in real time solely to detect and identify tarot cards. We do not use these images to identify people or faces.

B. Automatically Collected Information

  • Device & Diagnostics: Basic non-identifying technical data including device model, operating system version, app version, and crash logs to maintain app stability.
  • Purchases & Subscriptions: All financial and credit card transactions are handled exclusively and securely by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details. We receive only an anonymous transaction token and entitlement status via RevenueCat.

C. Cloud Backup Data (Optional)

  • Google Drive Sync: If you manually enable the cloud backup feature, the app interacts with your Google Drive solely to save or restore your encrypted database file in an isolated app-specific folder. We never access your credentials or any other private files on your Google Drive.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To generate personalized tarot card readings, daily predictions, and astrological context.
  • To recognize physical cards from photos using artificial intelligence.
  • To maintain your reading history, arcana frequency statistics, and energy balance.
  • To verify and maintain your active Aura Premium subscription.
  • To diagnose technical errors and improve user experience.
We never sell, rent, or trade your personal data to advertisers, data brokers, or third parties.

Note on AI Processing: AI queries contain only anonymized card spread information and query text. No personally identifying information (such as real name or email) is sent to AI language models.

4. Data Storage & Local-First Architecture

Aura Tarot employs a local-first architecture. Your profile, readings, and statistics are stored locally on your device in a private SQLite database. All network communications are encrypted using standard HTTPS/TLS protocols.

5. Permissions Requested

  • CAMERA: Used solely to take photos of physical tarot cards for recognition.
  • READ_MEDIA_IMAGES: Used solely to pick an existing tarot card photo from your gallery.
  • INTERNET: Required to fetch AI readings, verify subscriptions, and sync Google Drive backups.

You can revoke permissions at any time in your Android device's Settings > Apps > Aura Tarot > Permissions.

6. Data Deletion & User Rights

You can completely erase all your data at any time by:

  • Clearing application storage in Android Settings > Apps > Aura Tarot > Storage > Clear Data, or by uninstalling the app.
  • Deleting the backup file directly from your Google Drive.

7. Children's Privacy

Aura Tarot is not directed toward children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.
